The app can teach and provide provisional feedback while its quality is being evaluated. Generated material retains its provenance and uncertainty; curated sources retain their origins and rights. Editorial checks, trustworthy references, output testing and correction mechanisms matter, but there is no blanket requirement for a person to approve every lesson or recording before everyday use. Optional proficient-listener evaluation and held-out corpora test reliability; they are not routine duties assigned to a friend.

Claims of improved learning or native-like pronunciation still require observations of Ember’s learning and speech, not merely software completion, synthetic audio or model confidence. A transcript supports language-content feedback; it does not establish vowel quality, intonation or an accent. The native-like pronunciation ambition remains intact.

The current v3 UX is accepted, not merely its backend types: actual Opus/high outputs taught a lesson, correctly critiqued an answer unrelated to its exact question, and discussed explicitly adopted ASR words while acknowledging ё/e uncertainty and making no acoustic claim. The full run restored 24 events offline with zero browser errors. A real Qwen neural reference was also generated, linked, replayed and recovered offline. These are working capabilities; they do not establish the full completion contract or a pronunciation outcome.

The main local-Russian router is now active, with exact marked-stress→Silero provenance and no-inference ambiguity guidance; a single automatic short greeting is also accepted, with its acoustic interpretation still provisional. Actual generated audio still needs appropriate acoustic/listening evaluation. The current iPad v3/`.4` artifact now builds and matches server 3, but remains unsigned/uninstalled because Apple’s updated Program License Agreement awaits the user’s review/acceptance. The old incompatible v2 artifact is retained only as an archive. Physical runtime/Keychain acceptance, final Mac UI after desktop unlock, independent delayed learning, platform breadth and service recovery remain concrete outstanding work in [TASKS.md](TASKS.md).
