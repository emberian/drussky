Generated teaching can be used with transparent provenance and ongoing quality checks; no blanket per-item human signoff is required. Authentic sources/rights, synthetic voices, learner recordings, ASR hypotheses, confirmed words, self-reports and AI/human opinions remain distinguishable. Presentation is an opportunity, not proof of attention. A transcript supports language-content discussion, not vowel quality, stress, intonation or an accent verdict.

The current software provides useful accepted learning and sound-investigation flows. Completion still requires the remaining real-world/device/recovery work and evidence of learning; neither test counts nor a model’s confidence establishes improved learning or native-like speech. [TASKS.md](TASKS.md) owns exact receipts, historical test scopes and concrete outstanding work.

## Integration ownership

Root owns app state, navigation, Teacher/lesson/practice flows, shared manifests, consumer acceptance and final integration. Supporting lanes own learning facts/policies, curriculum, audio instruments/capture, storage/sync/recovery, teaching/speech providers and platform delivery. Shared-tree builds remain serialized. Actual implementations and observed journeys outrank a lane’s declaration of success.
