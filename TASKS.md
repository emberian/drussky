## Semantics to preserve

- v3 facts retain actual opportunities to see/hear material, original audio identity, independent transcript hypotheses and explicit learner adoption. Definition/import is not exposure. A first task input is not previous restudy; answer/reference exposure matters. Pre-log history can remain Unknown.
- Inline Teacher responses use exact context and `NotReported`, rather than manufacturing recall success/failure. Whole-response judgments are not copied into feature mastery. Explicit support and delayed/novel observations remain distinct.
- Per-phrase revision ancestry preserves all inherited `.3` authored v2 Events, hashes, practice keys and histories while new `.4` material extends the curriculum. Stress/meaning changes must retain separate explainable evidence.
- AI and human provenance, self-report, authored matching, ASR hypotheses, confirmed words and acoustic assessment remain separate. No mandatory human-review gate or homework queue is assigned to the friend.
- The interim contrast comparator is explicitly heuristic. Its diagnostic contracts/deduplication and completed-practice timing are tested; numerical forecasts are not POMDP optimality, a permanent learner diagnosis or measured learning gain.
