# Content provenance and review boundaries

Prepared 2026-11-05. The pack consists of original AI-generated editorial material.
It is not coach reviewed, a quotation bank, a certified course, or a native audio
reference. `GeneratedDraft` applies to all Russian items. The short checks below
support particular claims; they do not constitute a word-by-word linguistic audit.

Primary Russian language and institutional sources were searched for uncertain
sound/spelling and grammatical claims. Search-index passages were available, but
Gramota direct page requests returned HTTP 403 and Russian Corpus Grammar timed
out. These limitations are recorded rather than silently treating full pages as
read. Exact copied source prose is not included in the curriculum.

- [Lopatin et al., о — ё rules](https://gramota.ru/biblioteka/spravochniki/pravila-russkoy-orfografii-i-punktuatsii/bukvy-o-e):
  the indexed rule explicitly allows secondary-stress and unstressed ё in certain
  compounds and loanwords. The alphabet therefore says ё marks stress **in these
  examples**, avoiding the false universal “ё is always stressed”. Consistent ё
  remains useful and is never normalized into е.
- [Russian Language Institute spelling commentary](https://orfo.ruslang.ru/docs/biblio/russkoe_pravopisanie_s_kommentariiami_1.pdf):
  indexed primary-institute text describes vowel letters after paired hard/soft
  consonants, including loanword exceptions. Alphabet cues remain conditional;
  letters and sound categories are not presented as one-to-one mappings.
- [Gramota: повторить](https://gramota.ru/meta/povtorit): indexed dictionary forms
  confirm повтори́ть and повтори́те. The polite request is original teaching text.
- [2025 institutional Russian-language programme](https://iagprf.org/applicants/priyemnaya-komissiya/Inostra/ruslang-in-2025.pdf):
  its indexed programme lists accusative object and destination uses and
  prepositional location uses. Lessons teach bounded examples, not “every motion
  verb requires accusative”. Movement within a location is explicitly allowed.
- [Gramota reference answer 227061](https://gramota.ru/spravka/vopros/227061):
  confirms the particle/conjunction range of пусть. Mathematical let constructions
  are original supported bridge material and still require a specialist review.
- [Gramota: доказательство](https://gramota.ru/poisk?mode=all&query=%D0%94%D0%BE%D0%BA%D0%B0%D0%B7%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8C%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%BE):
  indexed dictionary and reference material supports proof/evidence vocabulary.
  No proof or philosophical position is attributed to this source.
- [Kazan Federal University, Sadykova, 2024 pronunciation workbook](https://kpfu.ru/staff_files/F_205786860/Sadykova_I._A.__Zanimatelnaya_orfoepiya_Praktikum_2024_2.pdf):
  indexed exercise states the standard чт → шт pattern in что and a listed set of
  related forms. The pack applies the note specifically to что and does not rewrite
  every ч or rely on spelling to assess spoken skill.

[Gramota dictionary entries for за́мок / замо́к](https://gramota.ru/poisk?mode=slovari&query=%D0%B7%D0%B0%D0%BC%D0%BA%D0%B8)
were checked for the castle/lock stress distinction. The Russian Language Institute
spelling commentary also explicitly lists лук / люк as a hard/soft spelling contrast.
Neither check provides audio. The host generates speech models for those tasks;
optional human recordings can supplement them.

The pack's mathematical statements avoid the disputed zero/one starting convention
for natural numbers: each holds under either convention. Written symbols are
preserved; no unreviewed speech rendering of `n + 1` is supplied. Logical and
philosophical comprehension items separate a stated proposition, offered evidence,
and a conclusion. That distinction is authored content, not an inference that a
source has validated Rufren's pedagogy.

Curriculum organization follows the repository's DESIGN.md and research reviews:
modality-specific evidence, contextual retrieval, explicit assistance, informative
contrasts, and probes on a changed message. No learning-rate or schedule optimality
claim is attached to these choices. Contributor review and actual use may change the
sequence, wording, and teaching approach.
