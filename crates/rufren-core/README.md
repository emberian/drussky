# Learning facts and policies

`Library` replays immutable facts. Current writes use event version 3 and sync protocol 3; valid v1/v2 events remain readable without rewriting history. Presentation, speech-transcription, transcript-confirmation, unrated-attempt, and confirmed-transcript AI evidence require v3. Older protocol clients must be rejected before receiving facts they cannot decode. `EVENT_VERSION`, `MIN_EVENT_VERSION`, and `SYNC_VERSION` describe compatibility; `SCHEMA_VERSION` is a legacy alias for the current event version, never a database schema number. Deterministic authored content can retain its original v2 envelope across app upgrades. Unknown versions/fields and inconsistent references fail atomically.

Human assessment is optional. A legacy-named `CoachJudgment` references an exact attempt and judgment dimension. Concurrent human opinions remain active until explicitly superseded; clocks never choose a winner. Annotations preserve an exact revision/response and optional UTF-8 byte span. Pagination may deliver references before targets: known and subsequently arriving targets receive the same validation. Missing references are not fabricated into evidence. Supersession cycles and unrelated parents are rejected.

`AttemptContext` preserves task, feature targets, exact content-pack/revision/exercise identities, optional effort and duration, and bounded saved questions. Backward-compatible `prompt` and `reference_answer` retain actual instructions, stimulus text, choice-ID/text mappings, and authored answer/example semantics (each at most 8,192 UTF-8 bytes). They survive a local session ending and synchronization to a device without the original pack. Literal choice IDs remain unchanged in `Attempt.response`. Missing historical questions are not guessed from current-pack questions. Legacy `novel_context` is an authoring claim, not proof of novelty.

`learner_snapshot` groups observations by feature, task and skill. A target tag does not establish that this feature was required, individually correct, or learned. Whole-response human judgments live in `FeatureEvidence.attempt_judgments`; `judgments` remains empty until a direct feature-linked judgment contract exists. Tagged whole-response correctness cannot inflate feature-specific delayed generalization. One attempt/time span may appear under several tagged features, so rows must not be summed as unique practice time or attempts.

`SpacedReview` is a transparent comparison baseline, not a fitted memory model. It schedules exact revision/skill pairs, starts an independent self-report at one day, advances only after the due delay, and bounds steps between ten minutes and 120 days. Cues and “with help” remain assisted. Early repeated success does not defer a review. Failure/help can shorten the retry. `SelfRating::NotReported` records an observation without inventing recall, failure or help: it never changes spacing, and the first later rated response still initializes the schedule correctly. Optional conflicting confident human recall judgments can trigger a short retry without rewriting self-reports. The confidence threshold and intervals are engineering choices, not calibrated probabilities. Future-dated attempts are visible but ignored until their timestamp arrives. Clock errors can affect due dates; they never decide edit or assessment winners.

`TeachingPlanner` is shared by the baseline and the optional `experimental-case-contrast-v2` comparison heuristic. It preserves candidates, selection, assumptions and overrides in `TeachingDecision`. Draft content can be taught by the AI; human signoff is not a prerequisite. The old `AskCoach` enum remains decodable but new decisions use AI explanation/representation changes instead of emitting that gate. This is not POMDP/lookahead or an optimized curriculum. Hand-set forecasts are explicitly synthetic calibration targets; comparator forecasts equal them, so no policy advantage is assumed.

Grammar weights require `GrammarDiagnostic`: an exact revision, task prompt, feature, source and distinct literal responses predicted by the case-based and first-noun-based interpretations. A subject-first item where both predict the same answer is rejected as nondiagnostic. Legacy response labels alone do not update weights. Repeated canonical utterances cannot multiply independent evidence in the current learning epoch. Observed linked instruction/answer presentation or completed matching teaching practice can start a new epoch. Source quality and likelihood-table uncertainty remain inspectable; these are hypotheses about current task responses, not stable traits or mastery.

Recommendations never count as learning exposure. A completed attempt must uniquely match a decision's session, policy, revision and skill. `StimulusPresented` records an actual opportunity to see text or acknowledged reference playback; it asserts neither attention nor learning. Audio text identifies intended reference content, not proof that sounds matched the caption. Failed play clicks, recording attachments and navigation intent are not presentation. Canonical token-boundary containment recognizes a short utterance inside a longer dialogue; punctuation, capitalization and stress marks cannot manufacture new material. Content definitions, including pinned v2 definitions, are not exposure. A prospective item can have no prior recorded exposure; a retrospective novel probe additionally needs its actual first `TaskInput` presentation. Legacy probes without that input log remain unknown. These conclusions concern the available saved app history only, never outside-app familiarity or an exhaustive account of earlier life. Reused probes remain useful retention practice.

`prediction_observations` anchors delay to observed linked teaching activity or completed practice, not the recommendation time. It requires suitable task/skill/assistance metadata and, for novel probes, observed presentation history rather than a novelty boolean. Optional human judgments, conflicts, self-reports and unjudged responses stay distinct. These are descriptive follow-ups, not causal estimates: transfer, other practice, source uncertainty and time trends confound comparisons. The ordinary AI teaching/feedback flow does not require human evaluation of these experimental forecasts.

`RecordingAttached` contains audio metadata and an exact revision/attempt link, not sample bytes. Reusing an asset ID with conflicting metadata fails atomically. SHA-256 identifies original encoded bytes; rate/channels/frames can describe a device decoding observation. Distinct asset IDs can share encoded bytes while documenting different device resampling. A link does not guarantee receiving-device bytes and never creates a pronunciation score.

`SpeechTranscribed` is an independent ASR hypothesis bound to an exact recording-attachment event, attempt, revision, encoded WAV hash and original half-open frame span. Full bounded worker JSON retains original/derived PCM identities, resampling, model/manifest/worker/decoding provenance, timing and usage; strongly typed fields must agree with it. The worker has no invented provider response ID or prompt version. Empty hypotheses are valid observations. `TranscriptConfirmed` separately records explicit adoption or editing of nonempty text, preserving the original hypothesis and conflicting confirmations. Confirming words is not confirming pronunciation. Original WAV-header coordinates must not be confused with a device-resampled playback representation. If the app converts WebM/MP4 to WAV, it must retain that WAV as its own correctly hashed asset/link before using this proof chain.

`AiAssessmentRecorded` is attributed model opinion. Written feedback retains exact immutable `Attempt.response` equality. Only an explicit `confirmed_transcript` reference can bind feedback to learner-confirmed spoken words while retaining the original recording label. The full confirmation→transcription→recording→attempt/revision/hash/span chain must agree. Text/transcripts support language-content feedback, never pronunciation, oral fluency or intelligibility verdicts. Provider/model/request/response provenance, corrections, suggestion, explanation and next step remain inspectable. AI opinions have a separate supersession graph and query; they never enter human-confirmed counters or silently change baseline scheduling. AI is the ordinary teacher; human feedback is optional.
