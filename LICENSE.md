## Third-party content

The app bundles recordings, texts, fonts and reference material made by other people. Each piece
keeps its own licence and its own credit. The complete inventory is `research/LICENSES.md`, with
one row per audio clip in `research/LICENSES-audio.csv` and per foundation asset in
`research/LICENSES-foundation.csv`. The app shows the same credits on its «Исто́чники · Sources»
page, generated from `apple/Drussky/Resources/credits.json` by `apple/scripts/build_credits.py`.
In outline:

- Human recordings of Russian words and sentences: Wikimedia Commons and Tatoeba contributors,
  under CC BY 2.0 FR, CC BY 2.5, CC BY 3.0, CC BY 3.0 US, CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 2.5, CC BY-SA 3.0,
  CC BY-SA 4.0, CC0 or public domain, as each clip's credit states. Share-alike applies to those
  recordings as adapted (trimmed, levelled, re-encoded), not to the application.
- Sentences: Tatoeba, CC BY 2.0 FR; the sentence author and translator are credited where Tatoeba
  records them.
- Narrations: LibriVox volunteers (public domain) and Wikimedia Commons readers (CC BY 3.0,
  CC BY-SA 4.0); one historic 1894 recording is public domain per the Commons template, with
  the US rule for pre-1923 recordings unverified.
- Literary texts: Russian public-domain works (author terms expired; first-publication facts
  and open questions are recorded per text in `library.json` and in `research/LICENSES.md`).
- Fonts: Marck Script, Caveat, Bad Script, Neucha and Comforter Brush under the SIL Open Font
  License 1.1; the full licence texts are in `apple/Drussky/Resources/Fonts/`.
- Reference material: Wiktionary (CC BY-SA 4.0) for reference forms and word origins.

## Models (non-commercial, share-alike)

- Synthetic speech is generated with Silero `v5_6_ru` (Silero Team), whose model and code are
  CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. That licence covers the model; whether each generated waveform is a derivative
  of the model is not established, and the project does not claim commercial freedom for the
  generated clips. Commercial use would need the Silero Team's permission or another engine.
- Phone recognition uses ZIPA `zipa-small-crctc-ns-700k` (Jian Zhu and collaborators; AnySpeech),
  CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, converted to Core ML. The converted model keeps that licence. It is not in git.

The NC and SA terms attach to those models and to adaptations of them, not to the source code.

## Model-authored content

Drussky's original lessons, explanations, exercises, stories and mathematics readings were drafted
with language models (Gemini 4 Argon and GPT/Codex drafts, edited by the project) and the live
tutor's replies are generated by Claude (Anthropic). This describes the creative process; it is
not a claim that the material is exclusively human-authored, and it gives no third party a licence
to it. Imported texts, recordings, reference material and fonts keep the rights and credits above.
