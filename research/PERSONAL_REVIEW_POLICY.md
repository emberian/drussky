# Personal review policy: bounded sessions, evidence and experiments

Research/design note, 2026-11-06. This extends [optimal learning](OPTIMAL_LEARNING.md) and [exact-task Teacher revisits](TEACHER_REVISITS.md). The current implementation batch is a bounded mixed session, qualified recent four-skill coverage and concrete reasons for selection. **No fitted retention model, predicted retention display or retroactive timing-policy change is part of that batch.**

Primary papers, repository snapshots and acquisition records are in `/Users/ember/paperbin/rufren/memory-scheduling-2026/`; `acquisition.json` records URLs, download failures and SHA-256 digests. `RECOMMENDATION.md` preserves the initial research memo. ## Decision

Build the bounded, balanced session planner first. Preserve exact-task baseline cadence and its support/timing qualification. After that useful baseline, a small, explicitly experimental memory forecast could run in shadow without controlling selection or due dates. A Bayesian half-life grid is a useful inspectable hypothesis for heterogeneous guided tasks. A pinned default-parameter FSRS implementation is a worthwhile comparator on compatible clean histories. Neither has been validated for Ember or these generated four-skill tasks. Their value is a falsifiable hypothesis, not a prerequisite for useful teaching.

## Primary evidence

- **FSRS:** the current SRS benchmark snapshot is 4ddb9fd9d6d7888ea52fc721d5ab0e6dd7a5e877 (2026-10-24). It uses ~727 million raw Anki reviews from 10k users, with chronological training/testing. The main non-same-day evaluation has 349,923,850 reviews from 9,999 collections. Labels are flashcard grades, binary recall treats Again differently from successful grades. This is prediction of future observed review responses, not a randomized comparison of learning gained per minute. The ABC wiki explicitly labels its 20–30% review-saving claim simulation-based. It still describes FSRS-6 as current (21 parameters), whereas the benchmark and fsrs-rs snapshot f0fbb3c0b9e3a2933aa466c4ab41911579055219 (2026-11-06) support FSRS-7 (34 parameters). Pin algorithm, parameters and input mapping. Current training.rs falls back below 8 training examples and avoids full training below 64 or with only first long-term reviews. These are implementation thresholds, not evidence that 64 sparse Rufren observations identify 34 parameters; examples can be repeated prefixes of the same card history.
  Sources: https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/srs-benchmark ; https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/awesome-fsrs/wiki/ABC-of-FSRS ; https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/fsrs-rs
- **HLR, Settles & Meeder 2016:** 12.9m student-word/session instances over two weeks; chronological first 90% training, last 10% test. Target is session fraction correct for words, with past exposure/success/failure counts and optional lexeme features. Reported MAE 0.128 versus Leitner 0.235 supports prediction in that setting. The later randomized 3.3m-user experiment's +12% concerns next-day app activity, not retained language. Lexeme coefficients had made some words decay frustratingly quickly; removing them improved engagement despite similar offline prediction. Do not infer every tagged word was recalled from one Rufren sentence grade.
  Source: https://aclanthology.org/P16-1174/
- **ACT-R, Pavlik & Anderson 2008:** 60 completers, 20 per randomized condition; 12 additional noncompleters. 180 Japanese–English pairs, three one-hour learning sessions, final test one week after last training. Typed recall/latency and fixed feedback timing support optimizing learning gain per practice-time cost against the tested flashcard/Atkinson controls. The model was developed using prior experiments, then adapted during learning. This is not evidence that response latency from interrupted iPad tasks measures memory strength, nor an all-language-skills result. Author-hosted PDF timed out and ResearchGate download returned403; the author-uploaded PDF full text was readable through web.open/find (methods and experiment sections inspected), so acquisition.json records failed downloads rather than claiming a local full PDF.
  Source: https://doi.org/10.1037/1076-898X.14.2.101 ; author upload https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Phil-Pavlik-Jr/publication/5261952_Using_a_Model_to_Compute_the_Optimal_Schedule_of_Practice/links/556491b108ae94e957204c6e/Using-a-Model-to-Compute-the-Optimal-Schedule-of-Practice.pdf
- **MEMORIZE, Tabibian et al. 2019:** stochastic control gives an optimal review intensity under exponential/power-law memory and specified loss assumptions. Duolingo observational evaluation uses ~12m sessions/~5.3m user-word pairs; filtering users and words to at least30 review events leaves ~5.2m pairs. This is not30 observations per user-word pair. Perfect session recall is binary success; sentence performance proxies word memory. Assignment by resemblance to alternative schedules is a natural experiment, not randomized deployment. Appropriate inspiration for a policy experiment, not a guarantee for Rufren.
  Source: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1815156116
- **SELECT, Upadhyay et al. 2021:** randomized question selection while learners choose session times/lengths, ~50,700 driving-test learners. After exclusions for reassignment and use shorter than two days, analyzed groups total11,481 learners (1,564/7,582/2,335). Reported ~69% longer retention is derived from normalized empirical forgetting rates after controlling review count/time, not a universal direct exam-score gain. Controls were random and difficulty-order selection; authors ask for stronger baselines. Early attrition was higher in SELECT. Its practical contribution is selecting within voluntary sessions, not dictating the user's study rate. Full primary PDF acquired.
  Source: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00105-8

## Observation contract

The prediction target must name its observation channel and task condition. SelfRecalled/Again without answer support can train reported recall; AI Correct/error on an exact unassisted response can train criterion-based task performance as a distinct channel. Neither is objective latent proficiency. AI correctness and accessibility of recall may disagree without either being a meaningless fact. NotReported is missing, not failure. WithHelp/replay/reference exposure is informative about support use and learning opportunities, not a binary failure label. A clearly timestamped unaided Again before help can be used as that earlier response; the current end-of-attempt aggregate does not establish this chronology by itself. TaskInput remains distinct from answer-bearing exposure. Unknown external exposure remains unknown. Grader versions and contested judgments must remain attributable. Confirmed transcript correctness is not pronunciation evidence.

Do not turn delayed same-task outcomes into feature mastery or new-context transfer. Do not manufacture Hard/Easy from reaction time, confidence, or AI prose. AI remains the primary teacher; sparse optional self-report only limits that forecast's coverage, not eligibility to learn or obtain a schedule.

For a Bayesian half-life hypothesis, define h as current post-learning state and p(t)=2^(-t/h). Update its distribution with a qualified response likelihood at actual review start, then apply a declared, versioned learning transition after completed practice/feedback. Multiplying all responses against one static forgetting parameter falsely treats a learning sequence as unchanged repeated measurement. Initially freeze learning-gain/process-noise assumptions rather than fitting many knobs; show sensitivity across plausible settings. Restudy supplies no success likelihood; its learning transition is explicitly an assumption. Preserve pending/future forecasts so late synced support requalifies labels without rewriting what the model predicted when.

## Product choices, not research constants

One Start action should offer a bounded mixed session: due exact Teacher tasks and generic reviews compete under the same plan, with recent skill coverage, relevance to current interests, and cost. Avoid making all four skills compulsory every day; balance coverage across sessions. Reserve room for fresh meaningful language so review backlog cannot consume the whole app. An example plan might be two useful returns plus one tiny story/dialogue step, but that count is a product default, not a scientific optimum.

Expose only useful controls: time/task budget; balanced versus chosen skill; lower-load today; topic/goal priority; pause a task; sooner/later/not today. Timing overrides alter availability/selection, never report a memory failure. Overdue means choose a useful next opportunity, not pay accumulated debt. Preserve the original policy due date separately from user snooze and session inclusion. Why-now should say concrete facts (last tried two days ago; previously needed a model; no listening practice in recent sessions), not pretend there is a calibrated forgetting probability. Help remains available and today’s lower-load choice is not a lasting learner trait.

## Lean falsifiable evaluation

1. **Future forecast test:** freeze a prediction before a genuinely new response starts, including model/prior/parameters, exact task, source history IDs, support condition, target label channel, and as-of time. Score only later eligible observed outcomes. Compare the experimental model against a pooled constant/recency baseline and a pinned FSRS comparator where mapping is defensible. Report coverage/exclusions, Brier and log loss, counts of successes/failures, and distribution of delays. Group uncertainty by task family/session; repeated observations are not independent learners. Train on the past only, then predict the next response; hold out the future, not random rows from the same history. No universal minimum sample count: twenty mostly successful responses can expose gross miscalibration but cannot establish small efficiency gains or identify forgetting/learning separately.
2. **Policy test next:** prospectively assign matched task families to baseline versus experimental selection/timing, retaining assignment. Use the same offered study-time budget, assistance policy and feedback quality. Count actual learning time including repair, but exclude network waiting/abandoned background tabs; report adherence and interruptions. Precommit a common delayed endpoint for each family (for example day14 from introduction) so a policy cannot win just by testing sooner. Randomization is a personal experiment, not a population trial. Siblings share a family because training one can aid the other. Compare retained outcomes per allocated minute, help use, time burden and whether the session was usable, not number of completed cards alone.
3. **Transfer separately:** reserve a small number of truly unpresented messages/speakers/combinations whose ingredients have been taught. Record first presentation and assistance, score the first response, then teach normally. Reused wording with a new UUID is not held out. Keep both same-task retention and changed-context performance: better recall of old answers alone does not fulfill the user's Russian goal.

Candidate falsification: a shadow model consistently loses to the simple forecast on future observations; a timing variant yields no better fixed-horizon retention at equal dose or causes substantially more help/burden; improved old-task recall does not transfer. Any can justify revising the model without blocking ordinary teaching. One or two tiny probes per week is a possible burden cap, not a statistically powered trial design.

## Clean failure probes and prospective timing snapshots

A clean failure is a committed unaided response or explicit inability to answer **before** a model, old answer, translation or corrective feedback becomes visible. Save the response boundary and exact support chronology. If the learner opens help first, the record is assisted/unknown; it cannot identify the probability of unaided failure. Conversely, a subsequently supplied explanation does not erase a previously committed clean failure. Optional help must remain available: preserve the observation boundary, then teach. A late `Again` report attached to an attempt containing support is insufficient to reconstruct the earlier boundary. Existing ambiguous records should remain ambiguous.

A future experimental policy needs an immutable decision snapshot, separate from both raw observations and the currently derived due date. At minimum preserve:

- Exact task/family identity, skill, actual review start and the decision's as-of time.
- Policy/model version, parameter/prior version, observation target and eligible evidence IDs; record excluded assistance and unknown exposure coverage.
- Baseline due date, proposed interval/due date or ranking, and the reason actually used; user timing overrides and preferences remain separate.
- Session budget, candidate set or reproducible candidate rules, selected tasks/order, experiment assignment and randomization seed if applicable.
- For a prediction, its probability/distribution and horizon **as issued**; for evaluation, the later outcome and requalification reason.

Late synced support can revise whether an outcome qualifies and can affect future planning. It must not overwrite the old forecast or make a historical decision appear to have used information unavailable then. A new policy must not silently reinterpret all past assignments as its own. The app can recompute a current projection while preserving the historical snapshot. This is an experimental design requirement for future timing comparisons, not a request to add a model or migrate cadence in the current batch.

Current coverage should likewise mean recorded opportunities/completions by skill, with support and source qualification where shown. It is neither four-skill proficiency nor proof that an omitted skill has been forgotten. A bounded session is complete when its chosen work is complete; a remaining backlog does not negate that accomplishment.
