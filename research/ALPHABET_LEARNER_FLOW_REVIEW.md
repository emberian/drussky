## Lesson development method

This method responds to the later actual-use rejection, which supersedes the optimistic sequence description above. It is a development decision, not evidence that the redesigned app already works. The whole Russian-learning objective remains unchanged; the first lesson is the reference implementation for extending the course.

1. **Define the capability and its prerequisites before the screen.** For a first short lesson: connect А/а and М/м with their demonstrated sounds, copy them, read the taught combinations ма/ам, then attempt a sound-to-writing response with help available. Explain precisely when a recording is a syllable rather than a letter name or isolated consonant. Plan a later return before showing the model. Separate recognizing, copying, recalling and saying; finishing is not correctness. These are proposed learning targets, not achieved outcomes.
2. **Storyboard every learner action.** Write the exact visible instruction, model/audio, expected response, help/error repair and next destination. Maintain one lesson context through teaching, paper, comparison and speech, with one primary continuation. Change the exercise within that context. Pause, reference and freely chosen practice remain available. Do not force every modality on every repetition or show every control at once.
3. **Review before expanding.** Root owns the integrated lesson. One independent reviewer checks Russian accuracy, sound/script distinctions, prerequisites and corrective explanations. Another inspects the rendered experience from the stated beginner task without receiving an implementation-specific click recipe. Both must identify the exact evidence for criticism. A model's existing Russian knowledge prevents it from serving as a genuine novice-learning experiment.
4. **Use three separate acceptance questions.** Teaching: was each required distinction taught and does help address a plausible error? Usability: can the next action and its consequence be inferred from the screen? Reliability: does the real sequence preserve ink, voice and place across pause/reload/offline transitions? Run authored-answer scripts as integration checks only. Inspect the rendered sequence and real audio, not only DOM assertions or step counts.
5. **Test actual entry histories.** Include a fresh course, an earlier catalog visit followed by the course, an unfinished catalog attempt reopened from the course, a sequence switch and a paused lesson resumed. Assert the intended lesson context and next target, not merely that a button navigated somewhere. These cases are chosen from the observed failure, not a request for a new general testing framework. Reuse bounded runners and warm artifacts; serialize expensive work.
6. **Separate implementation acceptance from learning evidence.** Before delivery, root walks the full reference lesson and an independent reviewer rejects unexplained transitions, missing teaching or misleading feedback. Native-only behavior needs native evidence. Ember's ordinary use supplies the personal learning evidence: occasional later attempts before help, in the intended skill, plus changed combinations. No assigned QA ceremony or friend grading. Failures revise instruction and support; there is no predetermined success quota or promise of optimal retention.
7. **Expand the validated pattern.** Once the loop is coherent, apply it to the remaining alphabet and meaningful words/constructions, reviewing prerequisites and representative exceptions. Keep two short records per lesson: intended capability and unresolved problems/evidence. Avoid another large framework or growing test counts as a progress surrogate. Any real-use rejection reopens acceptance.

The methodological sources are [Wiggins and McTighe's backward design framework](https://ascd.org/ASCD/pdf/siteASCD/publications/UbD_WhitePaper0312.pdf), [cognitive walkthrough inspection](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/cognitive-walkthroughs/), and the [IES learning practice guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1). They support outcome-first planning, examination of action discoverability/feedback, and teaching with examples, retrieval and spaced returns. They do not validate this exact adult Russian/iPad sequence or an optimal personalized algorithm. The more specific writing comparisons and their limitations remain in [HANDWRITING_ACTIVITIES.md](HANDWRITING_ACTIVITIES.md).
