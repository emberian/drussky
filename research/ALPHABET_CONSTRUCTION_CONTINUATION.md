## Sound, writing and recall contracts

Consonant examples are complete syllables or words. Instructions identify which part to notice and invite repetition of the whole model. No asset asks a consonant glyph's spoken name to masquerade as its consonant sound. This draft does not make memorizing 33 letter names a prerequisite for reading or speaking. Letter names remain a later reference capability to author separately.

The Е and Ы introductions point to a segment after an earlier consonant. Their standalone letter-writing targets cannot honestly use the current `initial_segment_of_syllable` focus. Instead the registry writes the already-meaningful complete **нет** and **ты**. Likewise Ь and Ъ have no standalone audio-writing tasks: the learner writes **соль** and **объект**, preserving the signs in the word. Й is modeled in its whole syllable and written in complete taught words. The glyph inventory still supplies both cases of every letter for optional visible-copy work.

Ordinary spelling stays separate from marked-stress requests. The ordinary targets retain Ё, Й, Ь and Ъ. Stress marks are teaching annotations, not extra handwriting marks. Existing `stress-acute-annotation` teaching precedes every stress-bearing note; requests and bindings keep the exact intended stress even when the learner's response does not require an accent mark. Examples explicitly tie reduction, softness, final devoicing, the normally hard Ж/Ш/Ц and normally soft Ч/Щ, and the pronunciation of **что** to their actual words. Their typography does not assess speech.

Each meaningful new presentation has See → Copy → Hear → Say and Hear → Write → Compare → Say routes. These are offered choices, not four mandatory repetitions of every item. Dictation must begin on blank ink with the model hidden. Save the original before comparison, then let the learner replay, compare or make a fresh try. Preserve motor form, orthographic content, meaning and speech as separate observations. A copied word is helped practice; a matching transcription is not pronunciation assessment.

The 49 exact recall/construction bindings identify the task and skill separately. Listening replay supplies the task's input; showing its text or meaning supplies answer-bearing help. For reading, spelling and meaning-to-speech tasks, revealing the answer/model before the response makes that attempt assisted. An immediate repair never becomes delayed independent evidence. Keep the original response, help chronology and repair ancestry, then use the existing scheduler only for an exact eligible later task.

## Independent editorial review, 2026-11-06

A second AI reviewed the Russian words, meanings, register, gender reference, person forms, lexical stress requests, phonetic explanations, task instructions and construction sequence. No lexical stress or conjugation error was found in the 92 requested models. This is textual review; every model remains unprepared and acoustically unreviewed.

The review repaired a specific overclaim about **объект**. Its hard sign reliably marks separation before the glide, but it does not require every accepted pronunciation to have hard Б. The [Большой орфоэпический словарь entry displayed by Gramota](https://gramota.ru/poisk?mode=slovari&query=%D0%BE%D0%B1%D1%8A%D0%B5%D0%BB) records both **о[б’j]е́кт** and **о[бj]е́кт**. That linked dictionary results page includes the объект entry despite its query spelling. The indexed dictionary text was available; direct page requests returned HTTP 403. The revised lesson explains separation, and the audition checks preserve both variants instead of treating softness alone as an error.

The explanations now distinguish the grammatical Ь in **читаешь / думаешь** from the softness-marking Ь in **соль**. Ш remains hard in those verb endings. This distinction follows [Gramota's explanation of the grammatical function of Ь](https://gramota.ru/spravka/vopros/294101). The lessons also explain the glide after a vowel in **моя / моё** and the verb endings, alongside their different after-consonant examples; see the [reference description of hard/soft consonants and glide notation](https://gramota.ru/biblioteka/spravochniki/russkij-yazyk-kratkij-teoreticheskij-kurs-dlya-shkolnikov/tverdye-i-myagkie-soglasnye-zvuki). These reference passages were read through indexed extracts; direct opens also returned 403.

Other repairs replace nonexistent highlighting with an explicit description of the upper/lowercase/example display, replace nonexistent hand/ball pictures with available or imagined referents, clarify the formal register of **объект**, remove doubled sentence punctuation, and give concrete comparisons for the person endings and new combinations. Handwriting instructions now explicitly distinguish writing the first consonant from saying its complete syllable; selected grammar notes accompany meaningful word/message tasks. Case-specific glyph hints replace shared hints that incorrectly asked lowercase **е** for three horizontal arms or uppercase **У** for a lowercase descender. No path coordinates were changed or visually approved.

Each audio request now names the lexical stress position in its multisyllabic words and adds the relevant word-specific onset, softness, reduction, cluster, ending or question checks. Stress annotations specify lexical position, not equal emphasis on every word in an utterance. The stressed practice syllable **не** is explicitly separated from the unstressed negative particle use. Ordinary spelling and marked requests still have identical letters after acute removal.
