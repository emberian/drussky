## Selection implications

**Keep human ю and ё as plausible glide-bearing candidates.** In [vowels.png](vowels.png), ю has a high-to-low resonance movement roughly 0.10–0.35 s, followed by the compact low-resonance vowel; у has a much steadier central speech interval around 0.23–0.52 s. Ё has a substantial movement into its stronger later vowel around 0.28–0.40 s. These are concrete onset contrasts, inconsistent with treating ASR's `у`/`Е` outputs as proof that their glides were omitted. The observations support teaching attention to the onset, without claiming confirmed /j/ segmentation or acceptable native realization.

**Do not reject у merely because ASR wrote О.** It contains a short steady low-resonance vowel. It differs from the established а/и spectra, but its low resonances and different recording conditions do not establish a reliable у/о classification. This remains the weaker unresolved vowel selection; a same-speaker independently identified у/о contrast or qualified listening would settle more than another isolated-letter ASR result. No replacement has been manufactured here.

**No obvious extra spoken labels in у or ю.** Their complete files are 2.247 s and 1.646 s, but each has one main compact speech island ending near 0.55 s, then low-level background/noise. Long duration therefore does not indicate the word `буква` or a spoken enumeration. This is an acoustic screen for extra speech, not recovery of the exact lexical content. Ё includes a softer onset and stronger later interval, with continuing spectral movement; its two energy rises are not evidence of two spoken words.

**Human лук/люк are distinguishable candidates; ASR collapse does not prove hard Л in both.** In [laterals.png](laterals.png), лук's main voiced onset near 0.34 s starts with a low candidate resonance near 0.8 kHz, whereas люк's onset near 0.03 s begins substantially higher and falls toward the vowel over about 0.05–0.20 s. This resembles the direction of the independently recognized synthetic RQ02/RQ03 contrast. It is a localized observation, not a universal F2 threshold: different source speakers/sessions, levels, onset durations and possibly recording chains are prominent confounds. Keep these as separately attributed whole-word examples; use the established synthetic pair where a controlled same-voice contrast is the teaching objective. Do not infer learner correctness from proximity to either recording.

**Synthetic моё: ASR's Майор has no confirmed extra rhotic event.** [my.png](my.png) shows two broad voiced regions joined by continuous resonance movement, with a smooth harmonic ending around 0.30–0.45 s and decay by 0.50 s. There is no obvious separate late interruption, repeated closure sequence, or additional speech island after that vowel. This fails to substantiate an added final /r/, although it cannot rule out a brief approximant-like realization or establish correct vowel/stress quality. `моё окно` has a different coarticulated first-word shape and subsequent consonant activity around 0.34–0.46 s; it is context evidence, not a byte-equivalent isolated model. Preserve the ASR discrepancy as unresolved lexical review, rather than silently calling this a correct pronunciation or automatically replacing it with the phrase/crop.

## Method and reproducibility

Run `scripts/research/inspect-alphabet-audio.py` with the existing `.local/qwen3-asr/venv/bin/python`, under `scripts/platform/run-bounded.py --max-mib 512 --seconds 90`. It imports only NumPy/SciPy and the existing offline `tracks` helper, with single-thread numerical settings. Analysis copies are resampled to 24 kHz, then use 20 ms Hann windows, 5 ms hop and 1,024-point FFT; plotted intensity is 2|STFT| sinusoid-relative dBFS with common −70 to −10 dB scales, without gain normalization. Stereo sources are averaged only in memory, explicitly recorded in [measurements.json](measurements.json). All source WAVs remain intact.

Candidate LPC poles reuse the existing 12 kHz, 30 ms Hamming, preemphasis 0.97, orders 12/14/16 method. Roots are hidden where local 20 ms RMS is below 5% of that file's maximum or 0.0001. Displayed roots are still model-dependent candidates, not established formants. The red curve is linear RMS on the right axis. Each row uses its own complete original-time span; widths must not be compared as equal durations.

`measurements.json` binds exact source paths/hashes, source channels/rate/duration and descriptive onset/following/late windows. Its active intervals use a 10%-of-maximum RMS level gate, not phoneme boundaries. The fixed-offset window summaries are reproducible aids to plot inspection, not a classifier or automatic alignment. Intermediate TSVs and gnuplot commands remain in ignored `.local/alphabet-audio-preparation/acoustic-review-v1/`.

The order sensitivity is substantive: human люк's 45–95 ms second-pole median is about 3.05 kHz at order 12 versus 1.64–1.66 kHz at orders 14/16; human у also changes second-pole identity across orders. Human лук's selected 360–410 ms interval is about 0.86–0.90 kHz across the three orders. This is why the visible resonance movement and localized comparison are more defensible than declaring every candidate second pole to be F2.
