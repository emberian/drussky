# Russian voice quality: next comparison, 2026-11-06

Rufren uses capable local neural TTS, but **we have not established that it supplies the best Russian teaching voice**. Full-BF16 Qwen3 VoiceDesign and Silero Xenia produced useful exact examples, alongside a numeric-agreement flag, a suspect contextual homograph contrast, and foundation-vowel concerns. Those actual results outweigh a model's size or reputation. Keep the working reference system and compare challengers against its retained outputs; do not replace the baseline on a leaderboard claim. ## Recommended candidates

**First local audition: OmniVoice BF16; quality-ceiling challenger: Drama 3 BF16.** This is an evaluation order, not a quality verdict. OmniVoice has direct Russian benchmark evidence, voice design without a human prompt, and an Apple Silicon path at much lower memory than Drama 3. Drama 3 offers a newer expressive/interactive architecture worth testing on the same material. Highest quality means selecting the better actual Russian output, not automatically choosing the larger model. Do not run a many-model download sweep.

## What to measure, and what decides the choice

**Pronunciation/meaning first.** Preserve raw audio and expected-free ASR. Count skipped/repeated words and changed values/forms; report `один/одна` separately from `21/двадцать один`. ASR cannot judge isolated vowels, resolve same-spelling stress, certify softness or prove ё from an е spelling. For contrast pairs, inspect the relevant original-frame region and perform blinded meaning/sound identification. Short isolated items remain diagnostics until their intended sound is supported; do not teach an unverified vowel because synthesis succeeded.

**Acoustics localize the question.** Use candidate vowel durations and stable spectral envelopes jointly for stress/reduction; exclude nasal/consonantal portions and record boundary sensitivity. Inspect lateral and nasal-to-vowel transitions for softness, not a whole-utterance similarity number. Track F0 through voiced regions with octave checks and original timing; do not join it across gaps. Russian question/statement distinctions involve pitch accent shape and alignment, not a universal “questions rise at the end” rule. Primary production/perception work supports evaluating communicative force separately from focus. [Meyer and Mleinek (2006)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378216606000427). Reduction and boundary lengthening also vary independently, so a long vowel is not automatically stressed. [Kocharov et al. (2019)](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2019/kocharov19_interspeech.html). Reuse the earlier [lateral](REFERENCE_QUALITY_SLOT1.md) and [stress](SILERO_STRESS_AUDIT.md) methods, without turning their two-token measurements into native thresholds.

**Naturalness is a separate listening result.** Randomize candidate labels/order and keep target text hidden during first-pass intelligibility/meaning judgments. Then reveal the intended reading for localized pronunciation review. Ask separately which voice sounds natural and which is easiest to imitate; a pleasant or dramatic voice can still teach the wrong contrast. A native/qualified Russian listener is needed before claiming native pronunciation/prosodic acceptability, ideally with a second independent listener for disputed cases. Ember can judge comfort and preference without being treated as a Russian gold labeler. No human verdict currently exists for the new candidates. Obtaining one is evaluation work, not homework imposed on the friend or a prerequisite for ordinary learning with existing usable material.

**Latency remains its own axis.** Record cold load, warm request-to-first-playable-audio, total generation time/audio duration, cancellation behavior and peak process/Metal memory on the actual M2 Max. Streaming provider latency excludes Claude thinking, endpointing, network and playback delays; measure conversational turn latency separately. Do not trade a target pronunciation error for a faster first chunk or average all dimensions into one “native accent” score.

A later authorized audition is bounded at **15 first-take outputs per challenger**, fixed voice/settings and no seed sweep or silent retry. The current baseline needs at most seven new exact items; the other eight are reused. Start with the diagnostic subset VQ02/04/05/06/09/11, inspect retained failures, then complete the remaining nine if the selected engine/runtime is viable. This partitions the same 15-item ceiling; it does not authorize additional outputs. ASR on lexical items is optional supporting evidence, not useful extra calls merely to recognize identical homograph spelling. All errors and unsupported controls remain in the result set.

Selection should name a scope: for example, “better natural conversation, retain authored Silero stress references,” or “better isolated sound examples, no latency gain.” Promote a challenger only after it improves the observed Russian problems without introducing new target failures. The working route can continue teaching while that comparison proceeds.
